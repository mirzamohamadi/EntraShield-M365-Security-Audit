# Remediation: Reduce Global Administrator Assignments

1. Export current Global Administrator members.
2. Identify active operational need for each account.
3. Replace standing Global Administrator roles with least privilege alternatives.
4. Use Privileged Identity Management where licensed.
5. Maintain two monitored emergency access accounts.
6. Review privileged assignments monthly.

Reference: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/role-based-access-control/security-emergency-access
